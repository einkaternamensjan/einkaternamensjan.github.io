# The Lake


The burning light upon the horizon,
Throws itself into this eye of mine,
A man who became himself unbelievable,
Spurns the sun, O sun of mine

Upon the horizon the grey ball,
Hides heaven and hell within its chest,
As guarantor for such a mound,
remains the horizon asunder and astound'

The tiny chamber with two bodies,
Full of love and full of tenderness,
In water the candle burns once more,
where the heart alone here splendidly blooms

The exit remains a stony gate,
Despite the face in moments of wrath,
They fall again the heavenly songs,
The rain remains their only bliss

White skull and black lake,
Never freed him from her gentle grace,
That which he is remains in her,
Alone and together in his chamber
